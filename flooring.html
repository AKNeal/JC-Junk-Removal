Interlocking Tile Floors

Heavy-duty interlocking tiles snap together right over your existing concrete, turning a cold, stained slab into a clean, finished garage floor.

  • Covers the ugly stuff. Hides stains, cracks and worn concrete.
  • Easy to live with. Damaged tiles can be swapped out one at a time.
  • Your look. Choose colors, borders and patterns to fit your space.
